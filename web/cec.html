About Us

The College of Engineering Chengannur commonly known as CEC, is an engineering institute in the state of Kerala, India, that was established by the Government of Kerala under the aegis of the Institute of Human Resources Development in 1993. The college is located in Chengannur, Alappuzha.

Courses Offered

Admission to the College of Engineering Chengannur (CEC) for undergraduate engineering programmes is primarily based on the Kerala Engineering, Architecture and Medical (KEAM) entrance examination and the subsequent centralized allotment process conducted by the Commissioner for Entrance Examinations (CEE), Kerala. Candidates who qualify for admission are allotted seats according to their KEAM rank, category, preferences, and the availability of seats in each branch. The total seats in each programme are distributed among different admission categories, including State Merit, reservation categories, and other applicable quotas as prescribed by the Government of Kerala and CEE. Candidates must participate in the centralized allotment process, pay the required fees, and complete the admission formalities within the specified deadlines to secure their allotted seat.
