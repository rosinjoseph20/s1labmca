FESTIVALS OF INDIA

India is a land of diverse cultures and traditions.Many festivals are celebrated throughout the country with great joy and enthusiam.Diwali,Holi amd Onam are some of the major fesitval of India.

1.Diwali

Diwali is known as the festival of Ligths.People decorate their homes with lamps,candles and colourful decorations.It symbolizez the victory of light over darkness and good over evil.

2.Holi

Holi is the Festival of colours.People celebrate Holi by playing with colourful powder and water.It is a festival of Joy,friendship and togetherness.

3.Onam

Onam is a famous harvest festival of Kerala.It is celebrated with flower decoration called pookalam,traditional dances,games and the grand onam Sadya.
